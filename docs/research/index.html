  • 2025 - 2026

    CITY4CLIMATE: Urban Digital Twins for Climate Action

    Researcher & Project Management Coordinator · NOVA IMS

    City4Climate develops a Digital Twin platform to support real-time climate governance in Lisbon, Porto, and Guimarães. By creating 3D virtual models that integrate live data and simulate scenarios such as emissions, air quality, and energy use, the project enables cities to make faster, data-driven decisions. Its goal is to accelerate the path to carbon neutrality while strengthening urban resilience and sustainability. funded by the Agência para a Reforma Tecnológica do Estado (ARTE), the Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia (FCT), and the Plano de Recuperação e Resiliência (PRR)
